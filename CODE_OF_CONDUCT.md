# Code of Conduct

citewatch adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text is at that address and applies to everyone who takes
part in this project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

In short: be welcoming, be respectful of differing views and experiences, give and accept
constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment,
insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a private report at
<https://github.com/biplob819/ai-visibility/security/advisories/new> (choose it even when
the problem is not a security one), or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub
profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines (correction,
warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) when deciding on consequences.
