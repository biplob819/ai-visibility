# M3.3 acceptance criteria

**HND-01** A seeded 50-URL sample stratified by effective source (proportional, at least 5 for each source with 5 or more pages) plus 5 undated pages; tuning-set pages excluded; drawn once.
**HND-02** A labelling sheet that hides the parser's answer, the source and the stratum order.
**HND-03** Error rate overall and per source with Wilson intervals; the held-out rule enforced by a test (a resolver change after labelling blocks publishing until 25 fresh labels exist).
