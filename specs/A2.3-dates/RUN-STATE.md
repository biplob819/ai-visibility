## Known limits
- The DAT-04 snapshots are normalised trims (date-bearing JSON-LD objects re-serialised, unrelated metas and markup dropped), so they test the resolver's choices, not raw-page parsing; the real-page shapes that broke parsing (no `@context`, control characters, nested images) are pinned by synthetic tests instead. `test_the_resolver_matches_each_label` passes on draft labels too: DAT-04 flips green only after `labels.json` has `status: confirmed` from the owner's review page.
- A site-wide footer like "Site last updated: <today>" is a visible-text `modified` candidate with no plausibility check, as §7.3 is written. More than 200,000 sitemap entries are cut. The `strict=False` on the first JSON-LD parse is masked by the non-strict fallback.
