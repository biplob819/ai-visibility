# Question rubric

Write this before any question, and keep to it (it is what makes the study fair).

1. Phrase questions the way your audience would ask them.
2. Name no website, organisation, brand, product or app.
3. One need per question, at most 30 words, no leading framing.
4. A question is time-sensitive when its right answer could change within 12 months; give the
   reason from `ts_reasons` in `study.yaml`.
